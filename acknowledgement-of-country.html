Acknowledgement of Country

Water Filtration Sydney acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waters on which we live and work.

Our work takes us across the Country of the Eora, Dharug, Dharawal, Gundungurra, Ku-ring-gai, Awabakal, Wiradjuri and many other Nations throughout Sydney and New South Wales. We pay our respects to their Elders past and present, and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.

We acknowledge that sovereignty was never ceded, and that Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have cared for these lands and waters for more than 65,000 years.

Why water matters here

Our trade is water. Clean water has been managed, protected and understood on this continent for tens of thousands of years, through knowledge of rivers, springs, soaks and seasons that long predates the pipes and meters we work with today. The Country we work on is criss-crossed by waterways that were, and remain, central to the life and culture of the people who belong to them.

We think it is worth saying plainly that the drinking water we filter comes from catchments and rivers that were cared for long before any of it was piped anywhere.

What this means for how we work

An acknowledgement is words. We try to back it with practice:

  • Treating every property and the people in it with the same respect, wherever we are working.
  • Working carefully on Country, minimising disturbance to ground and waterways.
  • Welcoming enquiries and applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people. See our careers page.

If we have got something wrong here, or you think this could be said better, we would genuinely like to hear from you: quote@waterfiltration.sydney.

Business Information

Water Filtration Sydney

Water Filtration Sydney is a licensed water filtration installation and servicing company operating across Sydney and New South Wales, Australia. The business is a NSW licensed plumbing contractor, not a retailer, and installs, services and repairs domestic and commercial water filtration systems.

Services include whole-house water filtration, under-sink water filters, under-sink reverse osmosis systems, rainwater tank filtration with UV sterilisation, bore water treatment and iron removal, water softeners and hard water treatment, shower and bathroom filters, benchtop and countertop filters, commercial and hospitality water filtration, and water filter cartridge replacement and servicing on systems of any brand.

Service areas include Sydney Metro and Greater Sydney: the Inner West, Eastern Suburbs, North Shore, Northern Beaches, Hills District, Western Sydney, St George, Sutherland Shire, Macarthur and Wollondilly. Regional New South Wales coverage includes the Blue Mountains, Central Coast, Newcastle and the Hunter, Port Stephens, Mid North Coast, Illawarra and Wollongong, Southern Highlands, South Coast and Shoalhaven, Central West and the Riverina.

All plumbing work is carried out by or under the supervision of Alexander Cossetto, holder of NSW Contractor Licence and Qualified Supervisor Certificate number 358626C. Installations use WaterMark-certified components to AS/NZS 3500 and carry a lifetime installation labour warranty. Emergency callouts are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
