Three Questions Decide Which System You Need

These are the same three we ask on the phone, in the same order.

1. Town water or tank?

Treated mains water is a taste, odour and sediment problem. Tank and bore water is a different job entirely.

  • Mains: sediment plus carbon does most of the work
  • Rainwater tank: heavier sediment staging, and UV where the water is drunk
  • Bore: the supply gets assessed first — iron, manganese and hardness vary property to property

2. Every tap, or just drinking water?

This is the single biggest cost decision, and it is about where the water is treated.

  • Drinking and cooking only: under-sink, in the kitchen cupboard
  • Showers, laundry, every outlet: whole-house, on the incoming supply
  • Both: whole-house for the property, under-sink or RO at the kitchen

3. Do you own the property?

Renting narrows the field, but it does not rule filtration out.

  • Owner: anything on this page is on the table
  • Renting: benchtop, or under-sink where the owner permits it
  • Existing system: skip ahead — you probably need servicing, not a new unit

Most Requested

Under-Sink Reverse Osmosis

The highest level of drinking water treatment we install: a membrane behind sediment and carbon pre-filters, plumbed into the kitchen cupboard with its own dedicated tap. It is the system most people mean when they say they want water that tastes like nothing at all.

  • Membrane filtration behind sediment and carbon pre-stages
  • Needs adequate incoming pressure — we check before quoting
  • Cupboard space for the manifold plus an 8 L storage tank
  • Sends a portion of feed water to drain in normal operation
  • We will talk you out of it if carbon filtration suits you better

What We Install

Every System We Fit and Service

Whole-house filtration

Point-of-entry on the incoming supply, sized to your flow rate and pressure. Sediment and carbon at every outlet, with isolation valves so servicing takes minutes.

Under-sink drinking water filters

Carbon block on its own dedicated tap. Reduces chlorine taste and odour, cartridges typically rated around six months, and it fits apartments where permitted.

Benchtop & countertop filters

No plumbing alterations, so they suit rentals and short leases. The compromise is capacity and bench space — we will say so plainly.

Rainwater tank filtration & UV

Tank water carries no residual disinfection and loads sediment fast. Staged filtration with UV sterilisation where the water is being drunk.

Bore water treatment & iron removal

Iron, manganese and hardness vary bore to bore, so nothing is specified until the supply is assessed. Staining and odour are usually the first symptoms.

Water softeners & hard water

Scale on elements, fittings and glassware shortens appliance life. We will tell you what softening will and will not fix before you spend anything.

Shower & bathroom filters

A targeted option when chlorine on skin and hair is the complaint but whole-house filtration is not warranted or not permitted.

Commercial & hospitality

Offices, cafés, restaurants and commercial kitchens. Specified from peak flow and daily volume, feeding coffee, beverage and ice equipment, serviced around your trading hours.

Cartridge replacement & servicing
