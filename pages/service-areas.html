Outside the Mains

Tank, Bore and Hard Water Need a Different System

A filter sized for treated Sydney mains water is the wrong answer for a property running off a rainwater tank or a bore. We size for the supply you actually have.

Rainwater tanks

Sediment, leaf matter and roof debris load a filter fast, and tank water carries no residual disinfection. Sediment staging and the right micron ratings matter more here than on mains.

Bore and groundwater

Iron, manganese and hardness vary property to property, so the supply gets assessed before anything is specified. Reverse osmosis is often the right call for drinking water.

Hard water

Scale on elements, fittings and glassware shortens the life of appliances. We tell you what treatment will and will not fix before you spend anything.
