The licence is held as a Contractor Licence and Qualified Supervisor Certificate. Anyone can check it, free, on the NSW Government register linked above, or search any licence at verify.licence.nsw.gov.au.

Connecting filtration to a drinking water supply is plumbing work, and NSW requires a licence for it, residential or commercial. If a quote you have received elsewhere does not carry a licence number, ask why before you accept it.

What compliance actually means on your job

  • WaterMark certified components throughout, as the National Construction Code requires for anything carrying or contacting drinking water.
  • Installed to AS/NZS 3500, the plumbing and drainage standard.
  • Certificate of Compliance issued where the work requires one, and a copy kept on your job file.
  • Backflow prevention matched to the hazard rating, registered with Sydney Water and tested annually where a testable device is fitted.

The detail, including which approvals apply to which jobs and who issues them, is on our Applications & Approvals page.

7. What we will tell you that costs us money

Transparency is easy when it flatters you. These are the ones that do not:

  • Reverse osmosis is often overkill on good Sydney town water. A well-specified carbon block gets most of the benefit for a lower installed cost, cheaper cartridges and no water sent to drain.
  • Some jobs do not need us at all. If your problem is a cartridge due for replacement, we will say so rather than quote a new system.
  • Not every emergency is an emergency. Call us at 2am with a spent cartridge and we will tell you it can wait, at the normal rate in the morning.
  • Performance depends on your water. We will not warrant a particular test result unless we have tested your supply and agreed it in writing.
