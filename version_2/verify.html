On Your Job

What Being Licensed Actually Changes

  1. Certified components, not whatever is in the van

    WaterMark certified components throughout, as the National Construction Code requires for anything carrying or contacting drinking water, and installed to the Australian Standard.

  2. Installed so it can be serviced

    Isolation valves either side, housings mounted where the sumps can actually be dropped at cartridge change time, and the system tested and explained before we leave.

  3. Your warranty survives

    Unlicensed plumbing work voids equipment warranties and is not compliant in NSW. Our installation labour carries a lifetime warranty while you own the system, and the equipment we supply carries a 3-year product warranty.

Transparency That Costs Us Money

Four Things We Will Tell You Anyway

Being open is easy when it flatters you. These are the ones that do not.

  • Reverse osmosis is often overkill on good Sydney town water

    A well-specified carbon block gets most of the benefit for a lower installed cost, cheaper cartridges and no water sent to drain. We will say so even though RO is the bigger sale.

  • Some jobs do not need us at all

    If your problem is a cartridge that is simply due for replacement, we will tell you that rather than quote you a new system.

  • A cheap unit is not cheap over five years

    Proprietary cartridges are where the money goes. We show the cartridge cost next to the installed price at quote stage so you can compare quotes properly.

  • We will not promise a test result we have not measured

    Performance depends on your supply. We will not warrant a particular result unless we have tested your water and agreed it in writing.
